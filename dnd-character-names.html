D&D Character Name Generator

Every campaign needs a name that sounds like it survived something. This generator forges character names in the classic tabletop register — half-elf ranger, dwarven warlord, or nameless thing from the fen — complete with an epithet your table will actually remember.

Press the seal to draw your first names.

How to use it

Pick a tone that matches your character's world — noble names lean high-fantasy and courtly, grim names suit a darker or more brutal setting, and wild names fit barbarian, tribal, or frontier characters. Leave it on "Any" if you want a mixed batch to browse. Draw as many as you like; there's no limit on how many times you can press the seal.

Tips for picking a good one

  • Say the name out loud before committing — anything your table will stumble over during a fight scene is worth trimming down.
  • The epithet doesn't have to be permanent. Plenty of tables let a title get "earned" mid-campaign instead of starting with one.
  • If you're naming an NPC rather than a player character, a shorter first name with no epithet often reads as more natural in dialogue.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of names does this generator make?

Names built from three tonal banks — noble and high-fantasy, grim and dark-fantasy, or wild and barbaric — each paired with an epithet in the style of a tabletop RPG character.

Can I use these names in a published adventure or novel?

Yes, generated names are free to use in any project, commercial or personal, with no attribution required.

Does the tone affect the epithet as well as the name?

Yes, each tone has its own matching set of epithets, so a grim name won't be paired with a bright, noble-sounding title.
