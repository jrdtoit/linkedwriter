Fantasy Map & Realm Name Generator

A map is only as good as its place-names. This generator produces kingdoms, wilds, and regions in the weathered, half-remembered style of a cartographer who's seen too much — good for a world map, a single road-sign, or a throwaway ruin your players will never visit.

Press the seal to draw your first names.

How to use it

Choose "Kingdom / Realm" for a settled place with a ruler and a history, "Wilds / Frontier" for untamed or contested territory, or "Ruin / Forgotten Place" for somewhere that used to matter and doesn't anymore. Each category produces a different title-and-place-name pattern, so switching between them is a fast way to fill out an entire regional map.

Tips for naming a map

  • Reuse the same root syllable across a few nearby names (a mountain range and the kingdom beside it, for instance) to make a region feel geographically connected.
  • Save the "Ruin" category for places your players will actually investigate — it's the most evocative but least useful for a background location nobody visits.
  • A realm's name doesn't need to be pretty. Some of the most memorable fantasy kingdoms have names that sound faintly unpleasant on purpose.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of place names can I generate?

Three categories: settled kingdoms and realms, untamed wilds and frontiers, and forgotten ruins — each with its own title and place-name style.

Can I use this for a novel's setting instead of a game?

Yes, the names work equally well for fiction, worldbuilding notes, or any project that needs a believable fantasy place name.

Will two different generations ever produce the same name?

It's possible but uncommon, since names are assembled from a wide combination of titles, prefixes, and suffixes.
