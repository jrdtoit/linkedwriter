About LinkedWriter

LinkedWriter is a collection of free fantasy name generators built for tabletop role-playing games, fiction writing, and worldbuilding. Instead of one generic tool that produces the same kind of output for every request, each generator on this site is built around its own dedicated word bank and phrasing style, so a dungeon name, a tavern name, and a character name each sound like they belong to a different part of the same world rather than the same shuffled list.

What this site offers

The site currently includes five generators: character names for tabletop RPG heroes and villains, realm and map names for kingdoms and wilds, dungeon names for crypts and ruins, tavern names for the inns adventurers pass through, and race or clan names for peoples and bloodlines. Every generator runs directly in your browser, produces results instantly, and requires no account, sign-up, or download.

How the tools work

Each generator combines a curated set of prefixes, suffixes, titles, and flavor phrases specific to its category to produce new names on each use. Results are generated locally in your browser rather than pulled from a fixed list of pre-written names, so repeated use produces a wide range of combinations.

Using the names

Names generated on this site are free to use in personal or commercial creative work, including tabletop campaigns, published games, novels, and other fiction, without attribution. See the Terms of Use for full details.
