Dwarf Clan Name Generator

A dwarf is introduced three times in one breath: their name, their clan, and the hold that raised them. The clan name does the heavy lifting — it announces the craft, the mountain, or the grudge before the dwarf says a word. Draw clan names by flavour, or full dwarf names ready for a character sheet.

Press the seal to draw your first names.

How to use it

Choose a clan flavour — mountain holds, hill country, the forge clans, battle clans, or the deep clans who never came back up — then choose whether you want clan names on their own or full dwarf names with a first name attached. Draw several at once: a clan name reads differently next to its rivals than it does alone, and the right one usually announces itself by making the others sound like somebody else's family.

Tips for choosing a dwarf clan name

  • Match the name to what the clan is known for, not what it does all day. Every dwarf clan mines; only one is the clan that held the gate for nine days.
  • A grudge name (Grudgeborn, Oathbreaker) implies a story the clan is still living inside. Give the player carrying it one sentence of that story and let the table do the rest.
  • Say it aloud in a shout. Clan names get announced across halls, battlefields, and negotiating tables — if it cannot survive being yelled, it will not survive the campaign.

How dwarf clan names work

Most dwarf clan names are two ideas welded together, and the weld tells you where the clan's pride lives. The first kind names a craft or a material: Goldhammer, Anvilfist, Steelvein. This is the clan saying judge us by our work — and meaning it literally, because a craft clan's name is a warranty. A blade stamped with a clan name that later snaps in battle is not just a broken sword; it is a stain on every living bearer of that name, which is why craft clans are famously slow to forgive their own apprentices.

The second kind names a deed, an oath, or a debt: Shieldbreaker, Oathborn, Grudgeborn. Somewhere in the clan's past is an event so large that the family renamed itself around it, and the name is a promise to keep behaving as if that event happened yesterday. This is the engine of half of all dwarf stories: a battle clan name is a compressed war story, and every member of the clan is walking around inside the sequel.

The third kind names a place: the peak, the seam, the flooded level, the door they guard. Mountain and hill clans do this most openly — Stonehold, Honeycombe — but the deep clans do it too, in their own register: Fathomdeep is a place name, it is just a place nobody on the surface has ever seen. When you are naming a whole hold rather than one clan, place names scale up best; when you are naming the family your character actually answers to at dinner, craft and deed names cut closer.

How to pick a clan name that earns its place

Start from the clan's reputation, not its trade list. Ask what a stranger two valleys away believes about this family before meeting one — that belief is the clan name, and the lists below are sorted to help: mountain for the high holds, hill for the farming valleys, smithing for the forge families, battle for the oath-bound, deep for the ones the surface only hears rumours about. Then pair it with a first name and say the whole thing the way a herald would. If you are building a whole people rather than one family, the race and clan generator names peoples, factions and bloodlines in a broader register; the individuals who carry those names can be drawn from the character name generator, and the hall they drink in belongs in the tavern name list.

Frequently asked questions

How does a dwarf clan name work?

A dwarf clan name is a compound of two ideas — a material, a place, a craft, or a deed — welded together: Ironmantle, Goldhammer, Grudgeborn. It works more like a banner than a surname. Every dwarf in the clan carries it, outsiders use it to place the dwarf instantly, and the clan's reputation, good or bad, travels with it.

What is the difference between a clan name and a surname?

A surname belongs to a family; a clan name belongs to a whole people under one hold or one ancestor, and it usually says what that people is known for — the craft they master, the mountain they hold, or the grudge they are still owed. A dwarf might be introduced by first name, clan name, and hold: three facts, no wasted words.

Can I generate a full dwarf name, not just a clan name?

Yes. Set the generator's output to "Full dwarf name" and it pairs a dwarf first name with a clan name, the way a dwarf would actually be introduced at the gate of a hold.

What are deep clans?

Our name for the clans who never came back up: dwarves who delved past the deep roads and stayed in the dark, kin to the duergar in most settings. Their names run colder — Nightwhisper, Umbralforge, Fathomdeep — and work for any underdark dwarf, exile clan, or family with something to hide.

Can I use these names in a published adventure or novel?

Yes, generated and listed dwarf clan names are free to use in any personal or commercial project without attribution.
