Fantasy Race & Clan Name Generator

Not every people needs a full mythology on day one — sometimes you just need a name that sounds lived-in, plus one line of flavor to build the rest around later. Useful for a species, a clan, a bloodline, or a faction with more lore still to come.

Press the seal to draw your first names.

How to use it

Each draw pairs a name — built from an elemental or descriptive root plus a suffix like "-kin" or "-born" — with a single line of flavor, such as a habit, a taboo, or a piece of folklore. Treat the flavor line as a seed rather than a finished fact: it's meant to suggest a direction for the culture without locking you into a full backstory before you need one.

Tips for building on the result

  • Pick the name and flavor line that raises the most interesting question, not necessarily the one that sounds "best" out loud — an odd detail is often more useful at the table.
  • A clan or bloodline name doesn't need its own physiology or homeland to be usable immediately; it can simply describe one family within an existing race.
  • If you're stuck on what a people's culture is like, try generating three or four and looking for a theme that repeats across them.

Frequently asked questions

Is this for a full fantasy species or something smaller?

Both. The output works equally well as a full playable race, a smaller clan or bloodline within an existing race, or a faction name.

What is the flavor line for?

It's a single trait or piece of folklore meant as a starting point, not a complete description — enough to build a fuller concept around later.

Can I combine this with the character name generator?

Yes, a common workflow is generating a people's name here, then generating individual character names to populate it.
